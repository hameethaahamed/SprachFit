Product concept: Goethe A1 Exam Readiness Checker
I would position this as an A1 Exam Readiness Assessment rather than another German-language learning application.

The learner has already completed A1 training. The purpose of this application is to help them answer one important question:

"I have completed my A1 German course. Am I ready to book the Goethe A1 examination, and what should I practise before taking it?"

The application should conduct a short diagnostic assessment, identify strengths and weaknesses, estimate examination readiness, and recommend the next 2–3 learning activities.

One important product decision: the assessment should distinguish between language knowledge and exam readiness. A learner may know vocabulary and grammar but still struggle with listening, writing or speaking under examination conditions.

2. Target learner and problem statement
Target audience
- Learners who have completed German A1 training.
- Learners preparing for Goethe-Zertifikat A1 (Start Deutsch 1).
- Learners who want to evaluate their knowledge before paying for an examination.
- Self-learners who need direction on what to revise.
Problem statement

After completing an A1 course, learners often lack an objective understanding of their readiness for the Goethe examination. They may know basic German but struggle to identify gaps in vocabulary, grammar and reading comprehension.

They need a quick diagnostic tool that translates their current knowledge into an understandable readiness assessment and actionable practice recommendations.
3. User journey
1. Welcome screen
   
   Learner confirms that they have completed A1 training and starts the assessment.
2. Diagnostic assessment
   
   Answer 10–15 questions covering vocabulary, grammar and reading.
3. Scoring engine
   
   Calculate accuracy by skill, identify weak areas and evaluate performance.
4. Readiness dashboard
   
   Show an overall diagnostic score and individual skill scores.
5. Personalised action plan
   
   Recommend the top 2–3 areas to practise, with exercises.
6. Reassessment
   
   Learner completes another short assessment to track improvement.

5. Scoring engine – how should the application calculate readiness?
This is the most important product decision.

I would avoid displaying only one overall score. The learner needs to understand exactly where they are performing well and where they need improvement.
Proposed scoring model
Each question carries one mark.
\[
\text{Skill Score}=\frac{\text{Correct Answers}}{5}\times100
\]

\[
\text{Overall Score}=\frac{V+G+R}{3}
\]

Where:
- V = Vocabulary score
- G = Grammar score
- R = Reading score
For example, imagine a learner receives:
Skill	Correct	Score
Vocabulary	4/5	80%
Grammar	2/5	40%
Reading	5/5	100%
Overall	11/15	73.3%

Suggested readiness bands
These are proposed product thresholds, not official Goethe grading categories.
Score	App interpretation	Recommendation
85–100%	Strong foundation	Complete full mock exam
70–84%	Nearly ready in assessed skills	Practise identified gaps
50–69%	Developing	Focused revision required
Below 50%	More preparation needed	Revisit A1 fundamentals

Important product safeguard: A 73% score on these 15 questions must not be presented as a 73% chance of passing Goethe A1. The assessment does not measure the official listening, writing or speaking modules. Call it a preliminary knowledge-readiness score and show those three examination skills as not yet assessed.
6. Personalised recommendations engine
This is where your product creates value beyond an ordinary quiz.

The system should not simply say "You scored 40% in grammar."

It should explain:
1. What the learner got wrong.
2. What concept they need to revise.
3. What exercise they should complete.
4. How they can verify improvement.
For our example learner:
Your personalised practice plan
3 recommended actions
1
Revise German verb conjugation

You struggled with the correct verb forms for ich, er and wir.

Practice: sein, haben, wohnen, lernen and kommen.

Suggested duration: 15 minutes



2
Practise German articles

Revise der, die, das and ein, eine, einen.

Practice: Complete 10 sentences using the correct article.

Suggested duration: 10 minutes



3
Practise everyday sentence construction

Form sentences using personal pronouns and common verbs.

Practice: Write five sentences about your daily routine.

Suggested duration: 10 minutes



Next step

Complete a short grammar reassessment and then attempt a full Goethe A1 mock examination.

The recommendation engine can initially be rule-based rather than AI-based.

For example:
Question tag	If incorrect, recommend
vocabulary.food	Food and drink vocabulary
vocabulary.time	Days, months and time
grammar.verbs	Verb conjugation
grammar.articles	Definite and indefinite articles
grammar.pronouns	Personal pronouns
reading.main_idea	Understanding the main idea
reading.details	Finding specific information

This makes the MVP predictable, testable and inexpensive to build.

Important assessment design correction
Keep the three categories mutually exclusive:
- Vocabulary: word meanings, everyday expressions, food, time, places and occupations.
- Grammar: articles, verb conjugation, pronouns, sentence structure and prepositions.
- Reading: comprehension, identifying details and understanding short messages.
For example, questions such as Ich bin 30 Jahre alt belong under grammar, not vocabulary.
